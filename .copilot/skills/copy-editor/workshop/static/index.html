Help

Drafts and findings

Plain text/Markdown with browser spellcheck, undo, autosave, and snapshots; not a rich-text or collaborative editor. Findings highlight the reviewed snapshot and any identical working draft. Edit this passage selects existing words without changing them. Resolved and Declined change only finding status.

Review freshness

Changing text, audience, or purpose hides draft highlights and requires new checks. Earlier findings remain on their snapshots. Restoring identical input reuses matching checks; changing a title or major-revision flag does not invalidate them.

Checks record successful passes on exact input, not resolved findings. Zero findings count; failed jobs, demo fixtures, and broad reviews do not complete individual checks. Saved snapshots have their own checklist.

External reviews and comparisons

Review externally copies a local-agent prompt or exports a complete packet. Import results returns findings to the reviewed snapshot. Local agents can import directly. Remote chats have no database access or cloud sync.

Comparison packets contain randomized candidates without revision metadata. Use a fresh evaluator with no editing history; importing JSON cannot verify its independence.

Privacy

Drafts and history stay in local SQLite; the server listens on 127.0.0.1. Sharing a packet sends its text to your chosen evaluator, which may consume usage. Copying or exporting does not call a model. No analytics or browser CDN requests.

Model limits

Prompts prohibit praise and rewrites. Schemas reject replacement fields and validate quotations, but cannot guarantee compliant advice. Nothing auto-applies; you can reject findings.

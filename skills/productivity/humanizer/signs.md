# Signs of AI writing: word lists

Full "words to watch" from Wikipedia's [Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing), grouped as in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). A match is a prompt to ask what fact the phrase carries, not a ban: context decides ("underscore" can mean an underline mark).

## Content

- **Significance and legacy:** stands/serves as, is a testament/reminder, a crucial/pivotal/vital/significant/key role/moment, underscores/highlights its importance/significance, reflects broader, symbolizing its ongoing/enduring/lasting, contributing to the, setting the stage for, marking/shaping the, represents/marks a shift, key turning point, evolving landscape, focal point, indelible mark, deeply rooted, [distinction] matters/is important because.
- **Superficial analysis:** highlighting/underscoring/emphasizing…, ensuring…, reflecting/symbolizing…, contributing to…, cultivating/fostering…, encompassing…, enhancing…, valuable insights, align/resonate with.
- **Coverage and attribution:** independent coverage, local/regional/national media outlets, trade publications, cited/featured/profiled in, written by a leading expert, active social media presence.
- **Identification by records:** databases/records/listings/filings identify/list/describe X as, was identified by [source] as, is listed in, his/her professional profile lists.
- **Vague connection:** in connection with, connected to, in association with, (particularly/widely) associated with.
- **Weasel attribution:** industry reports, observers have cited, experts argue, some critics argue, several sources (when few are cited), such as (before an exhaustive list).
- **Formula endings:** Despite its… faces several challenges, Despite these challenges, Challenges and Legacy, Future Outlook.
- **Biology and place writing:** belabored ties to "the broader ecosystem" and conservation efforts that no source describes.

## Language and grammar

- **AI vocabulary, by era** (clusters matter more than single words):
  - 2023 to mid-2024: additionally (opening a sentence), boasts, bolstered, crucial, delve, emphasizing, enduring, garner, intricate/intricacies, interplay, key, landscape (abstract), meticulously, pivotal, underscore, tapestry (abstract), testament, valuable, vibrant.
  - Mid-2024 to mid-2025: align with, bolstered, crucial, emphasizing, enhance, enduring, fostering, highlighting, pivotal, showcasing, underscore, vibrant.
  - Mid-2025 to mid-2026: emphasizing, enhance, highlighting, showcasing, plus the coverage words above.
  - Mid-2026 on: challenge, clearer, dependable, echoed, foster, leverage, matters, multifaceted, practical, prioritize, quietly, steady, universally; also heavy period and comma use with sentences held to about 8–20 words.
  - Grok-style: causal, empirical, correlate, underscore.
- **Copula avoidance:** serves as, stands as, marks, functions as, operates as, represents, boasts, features, maintains, offers, refers to (in a definition), began his career as / ventured into X as (for "was").
- **Negative parallelism:** not only… but also, it's not just… it's, not X but Y, no X, no Y, just Z, Y rather than X, and the multi-sentence form ("He came from a famous family. His life, however, took a different path…").

## Style and formatting

- **Promotional (older models, still common on request):** boasts a, vibrant, rich, profound, enhancing, showcasing, exemplifies, commitment to, natural beauty, nestled, in the heart of, groundbreaking, renowned, featuring, diverse array.
- **Summary closers:** In summary, In conclusion, Overall.
- **Templated personal pages:** About Me, My Interests, Let's Connect!, with emoji and bold.
- **Elegant variation:** a new synonym for the same thing in each sentence ("Soviet artistic constraints… state-imposed artistic norms… the constraints imposed by the regime").

## Communication meant for the user

- **Collaborative framing:** I hope this helps, Of course!, Certainly!, You're absolutely right!, Would you like…, is there anything else, let me know, more detailed breakdown, here is a…, check/verify/confirm… before publishing/submitting.
- **Gap and usage disclaimers:** While specific details are limited/scarce, not widely available/documented/disclosed, in the provided/available sources, based on available information, [claim] should be treated as… rather than…, does not by itself establish, should not be confused with, it is important to note.
- **Model self-reference:** as an AI language model, as of my last knowledge update, I cannot offer medical advice, but I can…
- **Placeholders:** [Name], [Year], 20XX-XX-XX, INSERT_SOURCE_URL_HERE, example.com/source1, (Add your URL here), "Add if available".
- **Service-desk register:** I understand your concerns, I have carefully reviewed, apologies for any inconvenience this may have caused, I am writing to…, I hope this message finds you well, if there are specific issues please point them out.
- **Truncation:** text that stops mid-sentence at the end of a long output.

## Citations

- Links that 404 and were never archived; DOIs that resolve to unrelated papers; ISBNs with bad checksums.
- Book citations with no page, or a page that doesn't contain the claim.
- Tracking parameters: `utm_source=openai`, `utm_source=chatgpt.com`, `utm_source=copilot.com`, `referrer=grok.com`.
- Reference lists whose entries are never cited in the text.

## Change summaries

- **Canned assurances:** ensured that… adheres to, refined, enhanced, enriched, streamlined, improved, in compliance with, [style/standards], clarity, flow, readability, neutral tone.
- **Procedural statements:** preserved/preserving, retained/retaining, avoided/avoiding, ensured/ensuring, aimed to.
- **Process over content:** added sourced/verified content, added coverage/citations, improved attribution, with independent/secondary sources; itemized parameter and template names with their markup.
- The more distinct "improvements" stacked in one summary, the stronger the sign. A short, specific summary with ordinary abbreviations ("ce", "typo") reads as human.

## Not signs on their own

Perfect grammar; formal, academic, or "fancy" prose in general; mixing casual and formal registers; bland prose; transition words in isolation; unsourced text. Leave these alone when rewriting.

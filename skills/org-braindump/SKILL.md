# Organize Braindump

1. **Start here:** name the single easiest first step across the whole dump.
2. **Topic buckets:** group related thoughts into separate headings (plus a **Quick / Unsorted** bucket for one-off items). Under each bucket, list its notes, concrete next actions, and open questions together.
3. **Parked / Someday:** collect ideas with no immediate action.

Keep every detail from the input without inventing anything or forcing unrelated items into one theme.
